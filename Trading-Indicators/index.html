Rule: 5% depth imbalance (bid vs ask within 5% of mid) drives the call — ≥ 2% bid-heavy is bullish, ≤ 2% ask-heavy is bearish; the 25% structural depth is a confirmation, not a trigger. Short-horizon (1h/4h) readings are ~coin-flip; the edge lives at the 24h horizon (see accuracy below).
