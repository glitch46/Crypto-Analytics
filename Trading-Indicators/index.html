Commercial Positioning (Contrarian)
CFTC legacy futures: commercial short increases often imply caution before mean reversion windows.
Commercials are typically hedgers, not momentum chasers. Caution Score rises when commercial short exposure expands relative to its own history. High Caution Score means higher probability of crowded longs and failed breakouts unless spot structure confirms. Use it with depth delta and CVD, not as a standalone trigger.
